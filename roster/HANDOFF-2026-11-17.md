## 4. Clock / sync

This is the part that has broken most often, so it is documented in detail.

### 4.1 What is in place

**Scheduled task `RosterClockGuard`**
- Runs `ops\clock_guard.ps1` as **SYSTEM**, highest privileges, hidden, **every 1 minute**
- Settings deliberately changed: **runs on battery**, **not stopped when going on battery**, `ExecutionTimeLimit` 5 min, `MultipleInstances` IgnoreNew
- Measures true UTC by SNTP: best-of-3 samples per server, then the **median across** `time.google.com`, `time.windows.com`, `pool.ntp.org`
- Steps the clock only when **|offset| ≥ 60 ms** (the bot's own gate trips above 100 ms)
- Refuses to act if the three sources disagree by >150 ms, or if a reading exceeds 5 s (bad data, not drift)
- Writes one line per run to **`data/trader/clock_guard.log`** — `ok N ms (limit 60)` or `stepped N ms (sources …)`

**W32Time** — `HKLM\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Services\W32Time\Config`

| value | original | now |
|---|---|---|
| `MaxPollInterval` | 15 (1024 s) | **6 (64 s)** |
| `MinPollInterval` | 10 | **6** |
| `UpdateInterval` | 360000 | **720000** |
| `MaxAllowedPhaseOffset` | 300 s | **1 s** |
| `PhaseCorrectRate` | 1 | 1 |
| service start type | **Manual, and it was found Stopped** | **Automatic, Running** |

### 4.2 How to check it in one line

```powershell
cd C:\Users\DELL\Downloads\saugan_pr\market_updated\roster
Get-Content data\trader\clock_guard.log -Tail 5       # a line per minute; a gap = not running
Get-Service w32time | Select-Object Status,StartType  # want Running / Automatic
w32tm /resync                                         # works only if that service is Running
```

A fresh line per minute means the guard is alive. **A gap is the alarm** — that is what happened twice.

Current state: guard log has 719 lines and **16 recorded steps** since install; offset is within ±25 ms.

### 4.4 Known limits of the guard

- It cannot correct **while the machine is asleep**. After a long sleep the clock can be outside 100 ms until the next tick (≤1 min), during which the bot will refuse to enter trades.
- It is the second line of defence. If it is ever disabled, the only protection is `w32tm`, which has repeatedly reported "success" while failing to correct.
- Practical advice: **keep the laptop plugged in during a run**; the guard is a net, not a substitute for power.

## 6. Known defects and open issues

### 6.1 `invalid amounts` — real bug, 13% of orders

The venue rejects a batch with:
> `maker amount supports a max accuracy of 2 decimals, taker amount a max of 4`

Cause: `trader/engines/speed.py` `_act()` meets Polymarket's $1 minimum with
`size = ceil(min_notional / limit * 100 - 1e-9) / 100`, which leaves **3 decimals on the USDC leg**. E.g. 9.1 shares × 0.11 = $1.001.

- Hit **6 of 38** orders in this session, 23 of 174 overall
- Only fires on cheap entries (limit ≤ ~0.22), which are the *negative-EV* bucket — so it currently saves money, but it is still a defect and it wastes a signal
- Fix direction: round the **USDC amount** to 2 dp and derive shares from it, instead of rounding shares

### 6.2 `FAK no match` — 40–60% of orders die at the exchange

`limit = min(0.85, ask + 0.10)`; we arrive **965 ms median, 2425 ms p90** after the decision (15% arrive past their own 2000 ms give-up). During that window the ask moves past the limit and the FAK is killed.

- **The 0.85 cap is the binding constraint on the best trades.** Orders capped at 0.85 missed 58% of the time, and that group wins ~95% of the time.
- Measured across the first 174 orders: filled **50%**, FAK-killed **37%**, rejected **13%**
- Missed trades were **not** adversely selected (64% win vs 66% for filled) — they were worth catching
- **`max_price` 0.85 is pre-registered** in `btc-speed-v4-bybit`. Changing it invalidates the test. At 0.90 you would need a >90% win rate just to break even, versus +0.75/trade at 0.85 — so this is a research decision, not a config tweak.

### 6.3 Fee model overstates cost

The venue charges **`fee_rate_bps = 0`**, but the ledger deducts `0.07·p·(1−p)` per share. Every live figure is therefore conservative by **~$0.08 per trade**.

### 6.4 Wallet balance reads can be stale

Observed at 20:41: read **$29.60** while the true balance 30 s later was **$23.37** (two fills had already settled). `live_session.py balance` refreshes via `/balance-allowance/update` but proceeds with the cached value if that call fails. Consequences:

- the launcher's "start cash" can be stale
- any cash-level watcher can miss or mistime a level
- it does **not** affect trading — the bot tracks its own ledger

### 6.5 Paper runs that are losing

`paper_v4sz50` (−36%) and `paper_v4sz100` (−16%) are new today and losing from the first trades, as is the live 0.75+ bucket. Treat size-up variants as unproven.

## 9. If you pick this up next

1. **Check the guard log first** — is there a line from the last minute? If not, find out why (battery? sleep? task disabled?).
2. **Check `audit.jsonl` for `"kind":"trip"`** — a HALTED kernel latches and silently stops trading; only a reset or a restart clears it.
3. **Check that `live.json` matches reality** — a killed process can leave it `true`.
4. The two code-level items worth fixing, in order: the **$1-minimum rounding** (cheap, contained) and then a **proper analysis of the 0.85 cap** (pre-registered, needs a decision).
5. Do not "fix" low signal counts. The detector was verified against independent Binance 1-second data: it fires on every qualifying ≥6 bp / 2 s move. Quiet periods are the tape, not the bot.
