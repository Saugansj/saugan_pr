# Paper runs: anomaly analysis, 15 Oct 2026

Runs checked: paper14, paper_v4b, paper_v5b, paper_ethb, paper_v6b, paper_eth6b (7-15 Oct). Read-only checks on each
run's ledger and journal, the Polymarket public tape (ops/real_outcomes.py), the 13-day BTC archive replay (research
loop, 500 ms) and the runs' own heartbeats, latency and pause logs. Script: scratchpad anomaly.py (not in the repo).

## Verdict

**The books are clean; the anomalies are in uptime, in what each run sees, and in how far paper is from live.**

| # | Anomaly | Size | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Runs can trade only part of each day | 52-74% of each day trading-ready (12-14 Oct), mostly PC sleep; of awake time 90-95%. A bug paused speed-only runs on ladder feeds (dvol, basis, the other coin): 1.2-1.3 h for v5b and ethb; **fixed 15 Oct 12:37** (trader/loop.py breaker_feeds) | **High** |
| B | Runs see different prices for the same signal at the same millisecond | BTC 58% / ETH 53% of shared signals differ by >= 2c, 7 / 11 by >= 10c | **High** |
| C | Paper fills are cheaper than real trades | Real takers paid a median 5.5-15c more than our fill; 30-39% of fills were cheaper than every real trade that second by > 5c | **High (known)** |
| D | Two realistic estimates disagree for BTC | Archive and best-case tape: +0.42 to +0.74 USD/trade; median-tape and live estimate: -0.02 to -0.34 | **High: BTC's real value is unknown** |
| E | BTC wins exactly as often as the real price implies | v4b won 60% at a real median price of 60%; v5b 70% at 70% (z 0.0 / +0.1) | Medium: no BTC edge at real prices |
| F | ETH's live profit is concentrated | ethb live +26.34, top 3 trades +12.09 (46%); eth6b +22.63, top 3 +12.51 (55%) | Medium |
| G | paper14's speed leg runs on a late feed | 50-100% of its signals skipped as stale on most days (Binance) | Low (legacy v2; ends 21 Oct) |

## 3. Same signal, different prices (B)

All runs of a coin decide on the same Bybit tick within 1-29 ms, yet:

| Coin | Shared signals | Asks differ >= 2c | >= 10c | One run traded, another skipped it as stale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BTC (v4b, v5b, v6b) | 33 | 19 (58%) | 7 | 7 of 48 |
| ETH (ethb, eth6b) | 49 | 26 (53%) | 11 | 5 of 174 |

Each run keeps its own Polymarket connection, and the Wi-Fi drops them independently (ethb: 395 reconnects on 14 Oct), so
each run's book lags by a different amount. Part of every run-vs-run difference is this, not the rule.

## 5. Which estimate to believe for BTC (D, E)

| Run | Archive replay (500 ms) | Tape best case | Tape typical (median) | Live estimate | Paper |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| paper_v4b (45 trades) | +0.433 | +0.423 | -0.077 | -0.186 (z -3.6 vs archive) | +0.621 |
| paper_v5b (37 trades) | +0.737 | +0.580 | -0.021 | -0.338 (z -6.3 vs archive) | +0.755 |

USD per trade. Paper matches the archive replay; the archive matches the tape's best case (we win the race for the
cheapest real price). The median and live estimates assume we lose it. The win-rate check sides with the pessimistic
view: BTC runs won exactly as often as the real median price implied (v4b 60% vs 60%, v5b 70% vs 70%), while ethb won
60% at a real median price of 54% (z +1.6), a modest real edge.

So **BTC's live value lies between about -0.3 and +0.6 USD per trade and these data cannot narrow it**; ETH is positive
under every estimate. The missing piece is volume: whether at least our 5 shares really traded at or below our price
after our order would have arrived. A volume-aware live estimate would settle it.

## 6. Other observations

- **ETH "already holding"**: on 14 Oct ethb skipped 75 of 170 market signals because it already held that market (one position
  per market). The 12 Oct loop priced these misses at about +14 USD (paper). It is a strategy question, logged here because
  it is the largest skip reason.
- **Drawdowns at live prices are larger than paper's**: v4b 14.78 vs 8.18, v5b 14.26 vs 7.25.
- **paper14 (G)**: stale skips 22 of 62 (8 Oct), 8 of 8 (11 Oct), 8 of 10 (13 Oct), 12 of 22 (14 Oct); its Binance feed is
  late. Its ladder leg is unaffected.
- **Latency**: Polymarket round trip 237-279 ms day medians; 07-11 IST on 14 Oct ~400 ms (Wi-Fi after wake). The huge loop
  maxima in the logs are the sleeps.
