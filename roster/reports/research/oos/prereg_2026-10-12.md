Why: v4 was chosen from 720 rules on 13 archive days (27 Jul - 9 Aug) and v5's calm filter was seen on the same days.

Data: Polymarket BTC 5m/15m catalog + top of book from the pmxt archive (history/pm_fast), Binance BTCUSDT aggTrades
(on disk), Bybit spot BTCUSDT trades (public.bybit.com). Replay: research.loop.nightly.wide + trades, unchanged code.
These days go to data/research/oos/ only and are NEVER added to the research loop's selection data (data/research/loop/wide).

Primary test (decides the verdict): v5's live rule, Binance-fed, replay delay 500 ms:
  2 s / 6 bp detector, ask seen <= 0.85, fill at the arrival ask <= min(0.85, seen + 0.10), BTC 30-min rv30 < 5.5 bp/min,
  one position per market, held to resolution, 5 shares, taker fee 0.07 x p(1-p).
  Statistic: fee-net P&L per trade, cluster SE by market, every day with complete data (24 archive hours).
  PASS: n >= 200 and mean > 2 SE.  FAIL: mean <= 0.  Otherwise INCONCLUSIVE.

Secondary (reported, never decisive): v4's rule (no calm filter); v5 minus v4 per trade; the same at 1000 ms delay;
v5 Bybit-fed; per-month means (to see edge decay over time).
