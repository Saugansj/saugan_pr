# Strategy research, 14 Oct 2026: what changed and why

Every candidate from improvements_2026-10-14.md was checked in independent data before anything was built:

- **Archive**: 13 BTC days, 27 Jul-9 Aug, v4's detector (2 s / 6 bp), filled at the venue's real arrival ask
  (research.loop.nightly.trades), at 200 / 500 / 1000 ms extra delay. The 13 Apr-26 Jul out-of-sample days were not
  used, so the pre-registered out-of-sample test (oos/prereg_2026-10-12.md) stays clean.

  Polymarket's public tape in our order's second, filled only within our limit, else $0).
