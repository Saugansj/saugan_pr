## Where money was missed today

**v3: actual −0.08; it could have made about +3.8, roughly what v2 made.**
- **0.75 cap:** it skipped 4 buys at 0.77–0.87 that all won (17:16 15m @ 0.87, 19:03 15m @ 0.81, 19:06 5m @ 0.77, ...). Cost about **2.5**.
- **Exit after 8 s:** its two 18:18 trades held would have made +0.89 instead of −0.08. Cost **0.97**.
- **Speed:** on the same 18:18:45 signal v3 paid 0.29 / 0.50 and paper14 paid 0.22 / 0.45. v3's Binance tick arrived about 250 ms later. About 6c a share, about **0.60**.
- **Stale signal at 17:16:** v3's tick was 1.9 s late. No cost, because the cap would have skipped it anyway.

**v2: actual +4.87, close to the most it could have made.**
- **Asks above 0.90:** 3 skipped buys at 0.91–0.98 all won, but pay little: about **0.79**.
- **No ask:** two 5m markets (17:02, 19:03) had no seller on our side. Not buyable: the market had already moved.
- **Exit choice:** holding was best. Exit 5 s would have made +2.26, exit 60 s −1.01.
- **Daily-loss pause from 19:17:** it tripped on positions marked at the bid that then settled in profit. No signal came after it, so it cost 0 today; on a busy day it would cost trades.
- **2c slippage limit:** didn't bind today.

## Lessons

1. **Holding beat the quick exit again** (2 of 3 days now, and in the history replay at realistic delay).
2. **Today was one-way: every expensive entry won.** Over 13 history days, held entries at 0.75–0.85 averaged −0.08 and above 0.85 −0.13. Don't raise v3's cap on one day. Let the nightly loop judge it.
3. **Speed is visible in the price paid:** about 6c a share between two processes on the same PC and the same signal.
4. **The $5 daily-loss breaker trips on mark-to-bid swings of held positions.** That suits a quick-exit strategy better than a hold strategy. Watch whether it costs v2 or v4 trades on busy days before changing anything.
