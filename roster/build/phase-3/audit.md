**Open defects at final audit** (none are blockers or majors unless marked):

- **minor**: basket() passes caller rows straight to evidence() without dropping rows with ts_ms > now_ms, so future scanner rows can open the gate. Probe: 30 events of rows at NOW+10s gave basket(...) reason 'ok' at NOW. In the wired path Ledger.as_of(now) prevents this. A basket replay or backtest that passes the full scan log would have look-ahead. *Fix hint:* In basket(): ev = evidence([r for r in rows if r['ts_ms'] <= now_ms], params['n_min']).
- **minor**: The gate and hurdle trust caller params with no floor. params n_min=0 opens the gate with zero rows (probe 7b: reason 'ok'), and a negative legging_budget lowers the hurdle (probe 9: 0.995 for raw asks summing to 1.005). This matches the spec, since these are registry parameters, but it is a soft spot in a safety gate. *Fix hint:* Clamp n_min to at least PREREG['n'] (30) and legging_budget to at least 0 inside basket()/hurdle(), or refuse params that violate these.
